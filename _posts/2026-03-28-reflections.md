## march 28, 2026

Welcome to Day 1. I worry about AI's effects on my cognitive ability - specifically writing, coding, doing math etc. Addmitedly, I got caught in the deafening hype/discourse on claude code and tried it for my own research. Surprisingly, it kind of worked and abosolutely smashed through multiple tasks I gave it. Until it didn't... Throughout this whole process however, what I did feel and continue to feel is a kind of awful brain-fog that I have never dealt with before. Embracing my inner vibe-coder left me at the end of each day with a gnawing, awful empty feeling - verging on hopelessness. I've seen Andrej Karpathy and the lot use different terms for this. AI-induced pyschosis is one of them. Im not even going to begin to address whether these terms are accurate but rather simply say, in theme, that they kind of capture the overall "vibe" of what people who use AI for knowledge-work and heavy intellectual tasks feel. Its almost certainly mischaracterized in my case, because people who use this phrase almost describe a kind of uncontrallable ability to be productive and build things. Not quite whats happening for me. But it would be unscientific not to seperate the effects of AI with other circumstances in my life. I just got into multiple PhD programs and I'm dealing wiht a kind of post-decision fatigue/emptiness which often comes when a long-term goal is achieved. Finally, combine this with how algorithms (social media) launch their own assault on my brain and its no wonder that I feel like this. Whats the point of this blog? Its to document how I will get out of this. I have certain ideas and we will see in due time what comes of it. Let the journey begin.
